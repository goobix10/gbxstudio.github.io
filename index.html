A LITTLE ABOUT ME

HOW IT ALL STARTED.

In 2023, I started gaming and watching YouTube videos. I enjoyed watching creators and their content, but there was something I kept wondering about.

How did they make those videos? How did they edit everything together and make their content look so good? I wanted to understand how it all worked instead of just watching.

That's when I started learning video editing. At first, I was learning how things worked, trying different techniques and getting better over time. I kept learning and exploring new ways to create my own content.

A few years later, I also started learning scripting for websites. I wanted to understand how websites work, how to build them and how to make my own ideas come to life. This website is part of that journey.

I've put a lot of time into working on this site, and I'm still learning and improving. There is always something new to try, whether it's editing, gaming or creating websites.

I hope you like my videos. They're down below, so check them out and please subscribe. Thanks for being here!
